The Origin Story

Why I Built This

"My mother died when I was 17. I have 10 memories of her voice."

I'm Peter. I build AI systems for Fortune 500 companies. ERP integrations, data pipelines, enterprise automation. That's the day job.

When my son Gian Lucca was born, I looked for something that could talk to him. Something soft he could hold. Something that could teach him Italian the way my mother taught me. Something with a real voice, not a robot.

Nothing existed. Not from any company at any price point. So I built it.

I built it so my son could hear me when I'm traveling. I built it so my mother-in-law could read him bedtime stories from Buenos Aires. And I built it so that voice, her voice, would never disappear.

Then I built it so every family could keep theirs.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Is it safe for my child?

Yes. Volume is capped at 85dB (pediatrician recommended). There is no camera, no screen, and no microphone recording when not actively in use. All data is encrypted. COPPA compliant from the ground up.

What happens if the company shuts down?

Unlike Moxie (which bricked all units in December 2024), Casa Companion has an offline fallback mode. Pre-loaded stories, songs, and voice content work without an internet connection. Your cloned voice data is stored locally on the device.

How does voice cloning work? Is it recording me?

You read 12 short phrases into the app (about 5 minutes). Our AI creates a voice model from those recordings. The model is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell or share your voice data.

What age range is this for?

Designed for ages 0-5+. The plush form factor is safe for infants (no small parts, no hard edges). The AI conversation grows with your child. A 1-year-old hears lullabies; a 4-year-old has real conversations.

Can grandparents really clone their voice?

Yes. Any family member with a smartphone can record 12 phrases through the app. The companion can switch between multiple family voices. Grandma in Italy, Abuela in Argentina, Dad on a business trip.

Do I need a subscription?

Basic features (pre-loaded stories, voice playback, offline mode) work without a subscription. The Family plan ($4.99/mo) adds AI conversation and learning modules. The Heritage plan ($14.99/mo) adds voice cloning and multilingual support.

How do I wash it?

Remove the electronics pod from the plush shell (magnetic attachment). The plush shell is machine washable. The pod wipes clean. Reassemble and you're done.

What languages does it support?

English, Italian, and Spanish at launch. French, Portuguese, Mandarin, and Arabic are on the roadmap. The AI can mix languages naturally in conversation.

When will I receive my Kickstarter order?

Campaign launches May 5, 2026. Estimated delivery: November 2026. We'll provide regular backer updates with manufacturing progress, prototypes, and timeline updates.

Can I buy additional plush shells?

Yes. One electronics pod works with all 10 plush shells. Additional shells will be available as add-ons during the campaign and through our store after launch.
